# Code of Conduct

Myo is a small, friendly project. The whole point is to build something kind and
human — so let's be kind and human with each other, too.

## The short version

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good faith. Everyone was new once.
- **Critique ideas, not people.** You can disagree — strongly, even — without
  being a jerk about it.
- **Be welcoming.** Harassment, discrimination, slurs, or unwelcome attention
  aren't tolerated here. Full stop.
- **Keep it constructive.** We're all here to make Myo better.

This applies everywhere Myo happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and any other project space.

## If something's wrong

If someone's behavior is making the project unwelcoming — or you're unsure and
want to talk it through — reach out privately to the maintainer at
**cpjeeves@gmail.com**. Reports are handled discreetly, and we'll do our best to
respond promptly and fairly. Maintainers may edit, remove, or block content and
contributors that don't follow this code.

That's it. Be excellent to each other. 🎙️
